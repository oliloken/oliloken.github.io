// 02 ABOUT ME

Behind The Lens

INSPECTED // FIELD OPERATOR
Oliver Løken ROGALAND · 58.97°N 05.73°E
"I'm not only there to take photos—I'm there to see how I can be of genuine value to you. I figure stuff out, stay calm under pressure, and work with what I have."

My interest in photography began in high school studying art and design. That was where I learned the fundamentals—lighting setups, deliberate framing, focus stacking, and flash photography.

Later, coming from a medical technology and engineering background gave me a distinctly practical edge. When an event is running fast and lighting shifts without warning, I don't panic. I stay independent, observant, and resourceful.

My favorite environment is covering live events: working quietly as a fly on the wall so attendees remain fully in the moment, while possessing the confidence to step forward and command group lineups when the agenda requires.
